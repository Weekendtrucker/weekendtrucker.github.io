Weekend Trucker — Members Mod Pack · Euro Truck Simulator 2

  • Welcome! This folder is my exact ETS2 setup, so you can build the same one. Two files:
  • 📦 Members Mods list — every mod I run, grouped by type, with a direct download link for each.
  • 🧭 ETS2 Load Order — the exact order to load them, top to bottom. This is the part that stops crashes.

How to use it

  • 1. Open the Mods list and download the mods you want — each link goes straight to that mod's page.
  • 2. Open the Load Order and set your in-game mod manager to that exact order — the mod at the top of the list goes at the top of your Active Mods.
  • 3. Load in-game and drive. That's it.
  • > 💡 Hate ordering mods by hand? The app Truck Mod Doctor scans your mods and sets the whole load order for you automatically — see truckmoddoctor.com.

Before you start

  • You'll want all 10 map DLCs for the full map combo: Going East!, Scandinavia, Vive la France!, Italia, Beyond the Baltic Sea, Road to the Black Sea, Iberia, West Balkans, Greece, Nordic Horizons.
  • A few mods are paid (marked £). Everything else is free.
  • Plugins (Snowy Moon, Cruise Control, Smoke) go in bin\win_x64, not the mod folder — see the Load Order file.

Stay safe downloading

  • Mod sites are ad-heavy and some fake "Download" buttons are malware. Run an ad blocker (uBlock Origin is free), only ever click the *real* download link, and scan every file before you open it.
